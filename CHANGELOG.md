# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-08)

- Rectangle is now the default snip shape. Freeform is one click away in Settings → Snipping.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-08)

First public release.

- Freeform and rectangle snips across all displays, on a frozen copy of the screen.
- Snips go straight to the clipboard and open in an editor.
- Editor:
  - pen and highlighter, with color palettes and adjustable size
  - eraser and crop
  - undo and redo
  - copy, save as PNG or JPEG, and print
- Pointers that show the pen, highlighter and eraser at their real size and color.
- Settings: global shortcut, snip shape, launch at login, auto-copy edits, ask to save edits.
