Simple Snip

Draw around anything on your screen to snip it, in any shape you like, then mark it up. A free, open-source snipping tool for Mac.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free · macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Coming from Windows?

If you used the Snipping Tool on a PC, you already know how this works. Press a shortcut, the screen freezes, and you drag a rectangle around what you want, or switch to freeform and draw any shape. The snip goes straight to your clipboard and opens in a little editor for highlighting, circling, cropping, saving or printing. macOS's own screenshot tool only takes rectangles and windows; Simple Snip adds the freeform snip and the quick markup step.

Install in a minute

  1. Download SimpleSnip.zip, open it, and drag Simple Snip into Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app, because Simple Snip isn't notarized by Apple yet (it's coming). Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Press ⌘ ⇧ 2, allow Screen & System Audio Recording when macOS asks, and reopen Simple Snip. You're set.

Prefer Homebrew? brew install --cask pavloff207/tap/simple-snip

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Simple Snip is open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Why does it need screen recording permission?

To take the screenshot; macOS asks that of every screenshot app. Simple Snip has no network access, so nothing it captures can leave your Mac.

How do I change the shortcut or the snip shape?

Click the scissors icon in the menu bar and choose Settings.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar icon and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
